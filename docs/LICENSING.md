# Licensing and distribution decision

K-NSFR is private. No license has been granted by this repository and no
external distribution is authorized by the setup baseline. Copyright and other
rights remain with their respective rights holders. Private visibility is an
access control, not a substitute for a license or a determination of ownership.

Before adding a LICENSE or distributing source, workbook, documentation, or
artifacts, the owner must record the rights holder, authorized license and
notices, intended recipients, redistribution conditions, and treatment of any
third-party material. Confirm that the chosen terms apply specifically to
K-NSFR. Record the decision and evidence in issue #3 and update this file and
any required notices. Until then, the license-selection criterion is open; do
not copy a license from another repository merely because the workflows match.

The inventory in [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md) identifies the existing imported
and authored surfaces. Future dependencies require a source URL, version or
commit, license text/notice, permitted use and redistribution review before
admission. A dependency manifest or vendor notice must accompany adopted code
where its terms require one.
