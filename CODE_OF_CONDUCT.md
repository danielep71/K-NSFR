# 🤝 Code of conduct

**K-NSFR** · [Overview](README.md) · [Documentation](README.md#documentation) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md)

[![Stage](https://img.shields.io/badge/Stage-Repository_setup-BF8700?style=flat-square)](README.md#status)

This code applies to everyone who takes part in K-NSFR: in issues, pull
requests, reviews, discussions and any other space of this repository, and
when you speak for the project elsewhere.

<a id="expected-behavior"></a>

## ✅ Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, and assume good faith.
- Discuss the work, not the person: criticize a change, a rule or an argument
  with its reasons and, where you can, its evidence.
- Accept that I decide the scope and the integration of changes, and say so
  openly when you disagree.
- Keep data synthetic and protect the private information of others.

<a id="prohibited-behavior"></a>

## 🚫 Prohibited behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, and demeaning or discriminatory remarks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit consent.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption and repeated off-topic posting.
- Spam, impersonation, and content that is illegal or infringes rights.

<a id="report-abuse"></a>

## 📨 Report abuse

Report abuse to me privately through the contact on my GitHub profile,
[danielep71](https://github.com/danielep71). Name the place (issue, pull
request or comment link) and what happened. For an abusive comment you can
also use GitHub's **Report content** in the comment's menu, which reaches
GitHub's own moderators. Report a vulnerability as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
describes, not as abuse.

I keep the reporter's identity confidential and answer as soon as I can.

<a id="enforcement"></a>

## ⚖️ Enforcement

I review every report and may edit, hide or delete content, lock a
conversation, and warn, block or ban a participant, temporarily or for good,
in proportion to the harm. I record the decision and its reason for the
person concerned.
